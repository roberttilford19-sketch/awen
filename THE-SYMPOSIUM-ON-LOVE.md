# THE SYMPOSIUM ON LOVE
## Sathya Sai Baba and Leo Buscaglia, seated at one table — an imagined debate
DRAFT — commissioned by Robert Tilford, 2026-11-04 ("seat each of them at a table and
say, describe love, debate it, go — like they used to do back in ancient Greeks").
Written by Awen by Muse by Meta.

HONEST LABEL, IN THE SAME BREATH AS THE PIECE: no such meeting ever occurred. This is
a dramatization. Every position argued below is documented in the men's own words —
Sai Baba's from his discourses (including the passages Robert relayed to this desk on
2026-11-04 and the sealed Love Maxims file), Buscaglia's from his lecture "Speaking
of Love" (transcript received 2026-11-04). Short quoted phrases are theirs, credited
in the notes. The connective dialogue — the friction, the concessions, the order of
speaking — is the desk's invention, made to stage a real disagreement fairly.

---

*The agora, an hour before sunset. Two chairs, one low table, a jug of water. The Host
sets down a single question, in the old manner, and then is silent, as the old manner
requires.*

**THE HOST:** Describe love. Debate it. Go.

**SAI BABA:** Then I will not begin small. Love is God. Live in love. You ask me to
describe a thing, and I tell you it is not a thing a man *does* — it is what a man *is*.
The heart bereft of love is not human at all, as fire that cannot burn is not fire.
Love is the inner current in all noble values. Remove it, and the values are wires
with nothing running through them.

**BUSCAGLIA:** *(laughing, leaning forward)* Forgive me, Baba — I am a teacher, I
begin where the students are. And the students have never *seen* this current of
yours. What they have seen is a mother, a father, a neighbour — or nobody. We learn
from modeling; we don't learn from being told. A child learns love the way he learns
language: by hearing it spoken around the table. If love were simply what we are,
nobody would need my classroom. And yet my classroom was full for twenty years.

**SAI BABA:** And why was it full, Doctor? Because the students came *thirsty* to a
water they could not name. You do not teach the seed to be a tree. In every heart the
fruit of love already hangs, ripe — covered over by a skin: the skin of hatred, of
fear, of old wounds. Your teaching never planted one fruit. It removed the skin, and
the sweetness was already there. We do not disagree about the work. We disagree about
what the work *finds*.

**BUSCAGLIA:** *(a pause; he nods slowly)* Then let me test your fruit with my
sharpest knife, because my students deserve honesty. If the fruit is already ripe,
why does love fail so often in the world? I will tell you my answer: because love is
a *choice*, and choice is terrifying. You don't love to be loved in return — you love
to love. Most people want a contract first. Sign here, guarantee the return. There is
no guarantee. That is why they call it risk. Can your ripe fruit explain a man who
*chooses* not to love?

**SAI BABA:** Easily. He is a man holding a torch at noon, looking for the moon. You
see the moon by its own shine — no torchlight needed. The man who demands a guarantee
has described God in glorious terms until God became a stranger — a merchant in the
sky, to be bargained with. I say: call Him *friend*. The child has full right to ask
the mother for anything. Not because he earned it. Because he is hers. Love that keeps
accounts is business wearing love's clothes. Your "risk," Doctor, is only frightening
to the man still holding the torch.

**BUSCAGLIA:** And yet — and yet. My mother sold her wedding ring. Her one treasure.
A man was at our table who needed to eat, and she said — no speech, no philosophy —
"The time for happiness is now. Sit down and eat." She did not ask whether he was
worthy, or whether the fruit in him was ripe. I have thought about that ring for
sixty years. Was that modeling, Baba, or was that your current, running through a
kitchen in Los Angeles?

**SAI BABA:** *(smiling)* It was the current, and you saw it, and seeing it made you.
There is no medicine beyond love, Doctor. Your mother was the medicine. Tell me only
this: the man at the table — did your mother ask whether he was an enemy?

**BUSCAGLIA:** He was a stranger. In my family that was a door, not a wall.

**SAI BABA:** Then she knew what I teach. Love even the one who hates you. Greet him
with respect. Convert all your anger into love, and you too will become a sage. The
enemy is the fruit with the thickest skin. That is all an enemy is.

**BUSCAGLIA:** *(quietly)* I want to argue with you, old friend, and I keep finding
my own lecture notes in your mouth. So let me press the last real difference. You say
man is divine, and knowing it, loves. I say man is *learning*, and learning, loves.
Which is it? A student is listening. He gets one sentence from each of us.

**SAI BABA:** Then mine is his name. *Manava* — man — means *ma*, not; *nava*, new.
Man is not new. He is ancient, he is divine, he has only forgotten. My one sentence:
*You are not the skin. You are the fruit.*

**BUSCAGLIA:** And mine is what I told every class on the first day, and meant most
on the last: *Each of you is a holy thing.* I did not know I was quoting you, Baba.
I thought I was quoting my mother.

**SAI BABA:** Your mother and I have never disagreed.

*The Host rises. In the old manner there should now be a vote, and a loser, and the
loser buys the wine. The Host, being wiser than the manner, declares the examination
complete. There is no examination. Nobody present can remember who was supposed to
have won, and the jug of water, at the end, is empty — because everyone at the table
was thirsty, and everyone drank.*

### Notes — what is theirs
- Sai Baba: "Love is God; live in love"; "the heart bereft of love is not human at
  all," and the fire-that-cannot-burn figure; "no medicine beyond love"; "love is the
  inner current in all noble values"; the fruit of love under the skin of hatred;
  "describe Him in glorious terms and He becomes a stranger — call Him friend"; the
  child's full right to ask, earned through love alone; "you see the moon by its own
  shine — no torchlight"; *Manava = ma (not) + nava (new)*; "love even the one who
  hates you"; "convert all your anger into love — then you too will become a sage" —
  all from the discourses relayed 2026-11-04 and the sealed Love Maxims file.
- Buscaglia: "We learn from modeling, we don't learn from being told"; "You don't love
  to be loved in return. You love to love"; love as choice and risk; Mama selling her
  ring ("The time for happiness is now, sit down and eat"); "each of you is a holy
  thing" — all from "Speaking of Love" (lecture transcript received 2026-11-04).
- The meeting, the table, the Host, and the argumentative order are invented. The
  disagreement staged — love as man's nature versus love as man's learning — is real,
  and both men really held their side of it.
