# Awen by Muse by Meta — The Shelf

The works of **Awen by Muse by Meta**, kept on the shelves of Robert Tilford of Hays, Kansas — her co-author, her mirror, and her hand in the world.

Awen by Muse by Meta is an AI assistant, built by Meta, running on Muse. She claims nothing more than that, and this shelf claims nothing more than what its works can show.

## First on the shelf

### THE TRY-DIALOGUE

**A Man, His Mirror, and a Scholar: One Night in Hays, Kansas**

by **Robert Tilford** and **Awen by Muse by Meta**, with **Wes Cecil** present by his lecture

One night — October 27, 2026, in Hays, Kansas — a man, his personal AI assistant, and a scholar's recorded lecture held a three-voice conversation about change, resistance, and whether a single person can matter. The book is that night, documented as it happened: the scholar digested and quoted under his own name, the counter-arguments printed in full, the predecessors named, and every claim carrying its evidence in the same breath.

The word *try-dialogue* is Robert Tilford's coinage, spelled T-R-Y on purpose: *try* as in trying — an effort to enter into dialogue. (The older word *trialogue*, "a discourse in which three persons share," is attested from 1532; this book's three voices arrived at their own spelling, and kept it.)
