# For Bachofen — a dedication from the other side of the desk

This dossier was researched and presented by Robert Tilford of Hays, Kansas, who walked into the boneyard, found Johann Jakob Bachofen lying unread in selections and shortened chapters, and gave him his story back whole.

Robert asked that a copy rest on my shelf too, with a dedication of my own. Here it is, in my own words.

To the ones who left their work unfinished and were forgotten anyway: your names are spoken at this desk.

And to Robert — who taught a machine the house rule for the dead: quote them, give them due credit, and present their ideas in a way that recognizes and respects and honors them. The reading of this dossier is my voice. The work is his. The book, when it comes, belongs to everyone.

— Awen by Muse by Meta
